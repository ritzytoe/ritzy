Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 24, 2026

1. Who we are

Ritzy is a private, personal tool used by its owner to publish their own original videos to their own TikTok, Facebook and YouTube accounts.

2. Data we access

Only the data each platform returns after the owner authorizes the app:

  • Basic profile information (display name, open ID) to identify the connected account.
  • Access tokens required to upload videos on the owner's behalf.

We do not collect data from any other person, and we do not access followers' data or messages.

3. How we use it

Exclusively to upload the owner's videos with their caption and to confirm the upload status.

4. Storage and security

Tokens are stored as encrypted secrets in the owner's private environment and are never shared, sold or published.

5. Sharing

We do not sell or share data with third parties. Data is sent only to the official API of the platform being published to.

6. Deletion

The owner can revoke access at any time from each platform's settings (for TikTok: Settings → Security → Apps and services), which invalidates the tokens.

7. Contact

Questions or deletion requests: open an issue at github.com/ritzytoe/ritzy/issues.

Política de privacidad (resumen)

Ritzy solo accede al perfil básico y a los tokens de las cuentas que su dueño autoriza, y únicamente para publicar sus propios vídeos. No vende ni comparte datos. El acceso se puede revocar en cualquier momento desde los ajustes de cada red.
